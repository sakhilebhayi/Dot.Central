This page explains the cookies Dot.Central actually sets. There aren't many — Dot.Central doesn't run advertising or analytics trackers, so every cookie below exists to make the platform work, not to profile you or serve you ads.

## Cookies we set

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `dot-central-session` | Keeps you signed in and remembers your session state as you move around Dot.Central. Strictly necessary. | Ends when your session expires or you sign out. |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | A security token that protects forms and requests on Dot.Central from being forged by another site. Strictly necessary. | Ends when your session expires or you sign out. |
| `remember_web_...` | Only set if you tick "Remember me" when signing in. Keeps you signed in on that browser after you close it. | Up to 5 years, or until you sign out or clear it. |

## What we don't use

Dot.Central does not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics or tracking pixels, or cross-site marketing cookies.

## Cookies set by connected platforms

If you use single sign-on to move between Dot.Central and another Dot Ecosystem platform, that platform may set its own cookies once you land on it — those are covered by that platform's own Cookie Policy, not this one.

## Managing cookies

Because Dot.Central's cookies are strictly necessary, turning them off in your browser will likely break sign-in and core functionality. Most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies through their settings if you'd still like to manage them yourself.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if the cookies Dot.Central sets change. We'll change the "Last updated" date above when we do.
